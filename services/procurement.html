How it works

1

You tell us what you need

Product specs, volume, budget, destination, and timeline. We don't need a purchase order — just a clear brief.

2

We identify suppliers and confirm pricing

We work with our supplier network in UAE and Asia to find the right match. You receive a sourcing proposal with pricing and lead times.

3

You approve — we place the order

Once you confirm, we place the order under BSA's trading licence and coordinate production or stock allocation.

4

Goods consolidated at our DWC warehouse

Multiple supplier orders consolidated into one shipment. Documented, checked, and prepared for dispatch.

5

Customs cleared and shipped

We handle export documentation from origin and coordinate import clearance at destination — or work alongside your existing customs contact if preferred.

6

Delivered — one invoice issued

Cargo delivered to your address. Single invoice covering everything — sourcing, logistics, customs, delivery.
