What's included

Full documentation

Cargo insurance

We assess and arrange cargo insurance for every shipment — including cases where clients haven't specifically requested it. Terms and coverage confirmed when we prepare your rate.

Customs on both ends

We coordinate clearance at origin and destination through appointed agents — you don't need to find or manage a customs broker separately.

Daily status updates

CEPA expertise for Ukraine

The UAE–Ukraine CEPA (2025) zeroes duties on 97% of goods. We know how to apply it correctly — so you're not overpaying.

Transport modes

Sea freight

Road and rail
