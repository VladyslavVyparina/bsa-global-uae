What we coordinate

BSA Special Project Logistics is a coordination and management service — not a heavy equipment operator. We work with vetted carriers, port agents, and ground handlers on each route to ensure the right resources are in place for your specific cargo.

Route feasibility
and assessment

Before anything moves, we assess the route — road clearances, bridge weight limits, port capabilities, and elevation constraints relevant to your cargo's dimensions and weight. If a route isn't viable, we find one that is.

Permits and
regulatory approvals

Oversized cargo requires transport permits at origin, transit, and destination. We coordinate approvals through appointed local agents — ensuring paperwork is in place before the cargo moves, not after.

Carrier booking and
transport coordination

We select and book the right carrier configuration for your cargo — flat-rack containers, breakbulk, low-bed trailers, or air charter for time-critical moves. Coordinated through our WCA Global network.

Exception handling and
delay management

When delays occur — and in project cargo, they sometimes do — we manage the response: alternative routing, carrier escalation, permit re-filing. One contact handles it, not a chain of intermediaries.

Pickup and
origin handling

Coordination of collection from origin, including liaison with site teams, loading documentation, and supervision coordination where required.

Customs clearance
coordination

Import and export customs clearance managed through appointed agents at origin and destination. All documentation prepared and submitted — including special declarations required for oversized or high-value equipment.

Real-time tracking and
milestone updates

You receive status updates at every key milestone — departure, port arrival, customs clearance, final leg. If something changes, you hear from us before it affects your timeline.

Destination coordination
and final delivery

Coordination of arrival, offloading, and delivery to final site. We confirm receipt and close the shipment with full documentation.

Vetted specialists on every route

We don't use the cheapest available carrier. Through our WCA Global network, we work with vetted partners who have experience with project cargo on your specific route — not a first attempt with your equipment.

One contact for the entire move
